# Wave P5 — requests-for-decision log

Deviations from `docs/specs/p5-rainfall-locusts-licks.md`, each with the measurement that forced
the call. Anything not listed here shipped exactly as the spec proposed.

## 1. Locust harness metric: cover *volume* removed, not cells touched

The spec's pass bar is "grass cover lost unmanaged ≥ 3× sprayed". Read as *cells with any eating*,
the ratio measured only **2.8×** (a swarm that nibbles a cell to 0.9 cover and one that strips it
to 0.0 both count once), and the cell count also includes cells the swarm merely drifted across.
Scored as **cover volume removed** (`eatenCoverTotal`, ΣΔcover over grass+shrub cells — the
literal "cover lost"), the same runs measure **65.2 vs 16.3 = 4.0×** with every swarm dead inside
its `days` in all variants. The volume counters live on `LocustSwarms` (`eatenCoverToday/Total`,
also in `report.locusts`); vacuity term `eatenCover > 0` (net coverLost is negative in both
variants — dry-season regrowth out-eats the swarm between passes).

## 2. Salt-lick sightings: the spec's fallback, taken with the measurement chain

Spec: traffic adds "nothing new unless the sightings measurement below shows emergence is not
enough". Three emergent designs were built and measured (sightings per tour-hour, control vs
with-lick, same seed, 20 tours):

| attempt | mechanism | control | with lick |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | animals wander bias only (0.6 retargets, ±2 m) | 15 | 15 |
| 2 | + lick placed 60 m from the road on the near side | 19 | 20 |
| 3 | + longer settle (1800 s pumped) so herds actually gather first | 25 | 20 |

Never a clear win — tour-to-tour variance at the demo park's herd sizes (~80 animals, 4-8 tours)
swamps a ±1-animal attraction effect. Shipped the spec's sanctioned fallback: **traffic sightline
radius ×1.5 for animals inside a lick's radius**. Clean run (8 fresh tours departing the gate after
a 900 s animals-only settle): **46 vs 38** (with-lick vs control; the pre-merge calibration
measured a tighter 44 vs 42 — tour-timing noise, same pass), control > 0 (non-vacuity), PASS. Two
measurement traps
found on the way, both now encoded in the scenario: `animals.update` is pause-gated
(`!world.time.paused || ctx.isShowcase`) so a `?speed=0` settle never moves a herd — the pump
unpauses `world.time` for the settle and re-pauses after; and the settle must be animals-only or
the tours burned during it deplete the day's sighting pool.

## 3. Waterhole building: checked, exists, not rebuilt

Spec §3: "If the player has no way to add water today, add a `waterhole` building request
(buildings) — check first". Checked: the buildings catalogue already carries pump/waterhole rows
(the demo park's wetland habitat has both), and `habitatStat`'s measured water term already feeds
`waterAccess`. So no new building was added; the harness variant instead *strips* the wetland's
pump + waterhole to prove the mitigation term (17 vs 4 deaths, §Shipped header).
