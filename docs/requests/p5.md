# Wave P5 — requests-for-decision log

Deviations from `docs/specs/p5-rainfall-locusts-licks.md`, each with the measurement that forced
the call. Anything not listed here shipped exactly as the spec proposed.

## 1. Locust harness metric: cover *volume* removed, not cells touched

The spec's pass bar is "grass cover lost unmanaged ≥ 3× sprayed". Read as *cells with any eating*,
the ratio measured only **2.8×** (a swarm that nibbles a cell to 0.9 cover and one that strips it
to 0.0 both count once), and the cell count also includes cells the swarm merely drifted across.
Scored as **cover volume removed** (`eatenCoverTotal`, ΣΔcover over grass+shrub cells — the
literal "cover lost"), the same runs measure **65.2 vs 16.3 = 4.0×** with every swarm dead inside
its `days` in all variants. The volume counters live on `LocustSwarms` (`eatenCoverToday/Total`,
also in `report.locusts`); vacuity term `eatenCover > 0` (net coverLost is negative in both
variants — dry-season regrowth out-eats the swarm between passes).

## 2. Salt-lick sightings: the spec's fallback, taken with the measurement chain

Spec: traffic adds "nothing new unless the sightings measurement below shows emergence is not
enough". Three emergent designs were built and measured (sightings per tour-hour, control vs
with-lick, same seed, 20 tours):

| attempt | mechanism | control | with lick |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | animals wander bias only (0.6 retargets, ±2 m) | 15 | 15 |
| 2 | + lick placed 60 m from the road on the near side | 19 | 20 |
| 3 | + longer settle (1800 s pumped) so herds actually gather first | 25 | 20 |

Never a clear win — tour-to-tour variance at the demo park's herd sizes (~80 animals, 4-8 tours)
swamps a ±1-animal attraction effect. Shipped the spec's sanctioned fallback: **traffic sightline
radius ×1.5 for animals inside a lick's radius**. Clean run (8 fresh tours departing the gate after
a 900 s animals-only settle): **46 vs 38** (with-lick vs control; the pre-merge calibration
measured a tighter 44 vs 42 — tour-timing noise, same pass), control > 0 (non-vacuity), PASS.
**(These numbers are historical: the counter took all species and the ×1.5 was later proven dead
code — see #4 for what the bar actually needed.)** Two measurement traps
found on the way, both now encoded in the scenario: `animals.update` is pause-gated
(`!world.time.paused || ctx.isShowcase`) so a `?speed=0` settle never moves a herd — the pump
unpauses `world.time` for the settle and re-pauses after; and the settle must be animals-only or
the tours burned during it deplete the day's sighting pool.

## 3. Waterhole building: checked, exists, not rebuilt

Spec §3: "If the player has no way to add water today, add a `waterhole` building request
(buildings) — check first". Checked: the buildings catalogue already carries pump/waterhole rows
(the demo park's wetland habitat has both), and `habitatStat`'s measured water term already feeds
`waterAccess`. So no new building was added; the harness variant instead *strips* the wetland's
pump + waterhole to prove the mitigation term (17 vs 4 deaths, §Shipped header).

## 4. ADDENDUM 2026-10-02 (predation-stability wave): the salt-lick sightings bar no longer holds

The predation type-III fix (branch `claude/predation-response`) reshuffled herd distributions, and
the salt-lick scenario's pass bar — sightings with-lick > control — now measures **false, and not
by noise**: staggered-tour measurement (8 independent samples instead of the one effective tour
the old pump produced) shows **control 39 / with-lick 38, ~5 per tour in both** (`perTour`
[5,5,5,5,4,6,4,5] vs [5,6,3,6,4,4,5,5]). The mechanism is intact — the herd genuinely gathers
(median animal distance to lick 3 m, 42 animals within 60 m) — but the ×1.5 sightline boost adds
~nothing at the spec's geometry: with the lick 60 m from the road and the base sight radius
already covering that distance, the boost only matters for animals in the 1.0–1.5× annulus, which
the gathered herd doesn't occupy. The pre-predation-fix passes (+2, +8) were single-tour coin
flips that happened to land right.

Making the spec's "sightings per tour-hour higher with the lick" hold again needs a change outside
the predation wave's scope (its rule: touch nothing outside the simulation module):

1. **traffic**: raise the lick boost (×1.5 → ×2+) so the gathered herd at 60 m gains visibility the
   baseline doesn't have — one line, but it is P5's shipped code, not this wave's;
2. **the P5 spec**: relax the bar to "not lower" (keeps non-vacuity of the control but the lick
   becomes cosmetic in the sightings channel — its real effects remain happiness + the visible
   gathering, both measured);
3. accept it as a known failing scenario until P6 or a dedicated pass revisits the lick design.

Option 1 was chosen by the owner on 2026-10-02 and executed on the predation branch — but the
measurement work first uncovered **two more P5 bugs**, both now fixed:

- **the boost was dead code**: the sighting roll pre-filtered its candidate pool with
  `animals.nearest(v, SIGHT_RADIUS)` (60 m) and only then raised the pass radius for
  lick-gathered animals — `dist ≤ 60` always, so the raised radius could never matter. ×1.5 never
  had any effect in any run; the pool now reaches the boosted radius.
- **the scenario counted the wrong thing and placed the lick off the wrong road**: the spec's bar
  is "sightings **of the lick species**", but the counter took every sighting — on the demo park
  34 of 40 events were wetland buffalo/rhino/hippo lining the route, burying the lick habitat's
  animals entirely (plains species: 0 sightings in both variants — the herd never comes within
  sight of a drivable segment). The lick also anchored to the globally-nearest road edge, which the
  tours need not drive.

With the pool fix, the boost raised to ×2 (120 m ≈ 208 m of visible road vs ×1.5's ≈ 134 m), the
species filter, route-edge placement, and the staggered 8-tour sampling, the habitat choice is the
last free variable and it is decisive — measured with-lick vs control:

| lick habitat | control | with-lick | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plains | 0 | 0 | herd never within sight of any drivable segment |
| Wetland | 12 | 8 | gathers only 6 (small grazer pool) while draining the roadside herd |
| **Kopje** | **17** | **22** | the predation fix grew its impala herd (14→25) while its roadside baseline stayed low — gathering into the boosted corridor adds |

`salt-lick` now ships with the kopje lick (PASS, deterministic per seed; `perTour` arrays in the
JSON), and the scenario's non-vacuity clause (control > 0 of the lick species) doubles as the
route-visibility sanity check the spec always implied.

**Measured on THIS branch (`claude/p5-rainfall`, without the predation fix — the fix commit was
cherry-picked here on 2026-10-04 so the verifier sees a green scenario without waiting for #33):
control 4 vs with-lick 12, PASS** — without predation the kopje's impala herd is smaller, so the
control baseline is tiny and the lick's gathered corridor stands out even more than the 22 vs 17
measured with predation. Also on this branch since the verifier report: the salt-lick mesh was
enlarged and re-contrasted (block 0.6 m → 1.35 m tall, sun-bleached disc / dark rust block — the
"small grey blob" at 55 m is now a clearly readable man-sized object), and the night swarm lost
its moon forward-scatter glow (flat scatter-free light — see the effects README).

## 5. RESOLVED 2026-10-04 — licks make no sightings claim (owner decision)

The owner chose to make the effect real (lick 25–40 m from the road, 3-seed margin bar), and the
measurement refuted it: on the final stacked state seed 2 measured **16 with-lick vs 22 control** even
at 25–40 m, after 2 vs 17 / 16 vs 16 on this branch at >= 55 m. Cause: a sighting is a stop along the
route, and gathering a roadside herd onto one spot removes stops; the lick only helps a herd that
started out of sight. Owner then chose to **drop the sightings claim**:

- traffic: the lick boost and the widened candidate pool are removed — `checkSighting` is identical to
  its pre-P5 form;
- harness `salt-lick` gates on what licks do — herd gathering at the lick and grazer/mixed happiness
  (+0.03 target per lick) — on the same seed with vs without;
- known side effect, documented: a lick that pulls a roadside herd away can lower sightings on that
  stretch of road.
